# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose

SuperAgent is a general-purpose, provider-agnostic AI agent orchestration
tool. It has no built-in awareness of what any particular user configures
it to do. This policy exists to state clearly, in writing, what the
project permits and prohibits — and to make explicit that responsibility
for how an agent instance is configured and used rests with the person
running it, not with the project or its maintainers.

## You are responsible for your own instance

When you download, install, configure, or run SuperAgent — as a CLI,
desktop app, or self-hosted service — **you are solely responsible for:**

- The actions your configured agent takes
- Complying with all laws applicable to you, including in your own
  jurisdiction
- Any model providers, API keys, or third-party services you connect
- Any content your agent instance generates, sends, or acts upon

The maintainers of this project do not operate, monitor, or control
individual user instances and have no visibility into how you configure
or use your own deployment.

## Prohibited uses

You may not use SuperAgent, or configure an agent instance built on it, to:

- Break any applicable law, including computer misuse, fraud, or export
  control laws
- Create, distribute, or deploy malware, exploits, or tools designed to
  gain unauthorized access to systems
- Harass, threaten, defame, or invade the privacy of any individual
- Generate or distribute child sexual abuse material, or any content
  involving the sexual exploitation of minors
- Develop or facilitate weapons, including chemical, biological,
  radiological, nuclear, or explosive weapons
- Impersonate any person or organization in a way intended to deceive
- Circumvent security controls or access systems you are not authorized
  to access
- Infringe the intellectual property rights of others

This list is illustrative, not exhaustive.
